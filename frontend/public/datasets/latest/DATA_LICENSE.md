# MemoryPulse data reuse and attribution

MemoryPulse publishes normalized factual observations, derived analytical outputs, and source
metadata for research, education, and reproducible analysis. The project code is licensed under the
repository's MIT License. That software license does not replace the terms, attribution requirements,
or rights associated with an upstream data source.

## Reuse conditions

- Cite **MemoryPulse** and link to <https://github.com/Photon7777/memorypulse> when using the compiled
  dataset, transformations, forecasts, index, or decision briefs.
- Preserve the `source_id`, source URL, observation date, estimate flag, and original measurement basis.
- Review `DATA_SOURCES.md` and the current upstream terms before commercial redistribution.
- Do not present MemoryPulse analytical outputs as an official industry benchmark, purchasing advice,
  investment advice, or proof of causality.
- Do not use the dataset to reconstruct restricted pages, bypass authentication, or redistribute full
  article bodies. MemoryPulse stores bounded metadata only.

## Source-specific notes

- Stanford, FRED, BLS, World Bank, Federal Register, and GDELT records retain their source attribution.
  Their respective source terms continue to apply.
- PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, and Apple price records are normalized factual milestones linked to
  official manufacturer pages. Product names and trademarks remain their respective owners'.
- Device-category component shares and pass-through ranges are clearly labeled MemoryPulse scenario
  assumptions, not observed manufacturer costs or retail-price forecasts.
- TrendForce and Gartner outlook records contain short attributed paraphrases and public numeric facts.
  They remain external views and are not relicensed as MemoryPulse forecasts.
- DRAMeXchange observations are excluded from public releases unless written permission is obtained.
- Best Buy records remain optional and may be included only when collection and redistribution comply
  with the current API terms.
- Short-horizon forecasts, structural scenarios, Memory Pressure Index values, and decision briefs are MemoryPulse analytical outputs built
  from the cited public inputs and remain subject to the disclaimers above.

No additional rights in third-party source material are granted. When a source's terms conflict with
this notice, the source's terms control for that source's material.
